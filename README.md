# Android Tiers
Android Tiers aims to add several tiers of sentient android colonists, robots, mechs, and advanced technological systems to vastly extend late-game colonies.

See the Change Notes tab in [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/changelog/1386412863) or the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/Atla55/Android-Tiers-Core) for info on the latest updates.

## Features

* **2 New Android Factions**: Android Insurrection and the Android Collective
* **70 Android backstories**
* **5 Tiers of standard android colonists** (T1 through T5)
* **Integrated TX Series Infiltration Androids** (TX2, TX2K, TX3, TX4 and terror infantry "I" variants)
* **2 Mechs and 5 Mech Weapons** (M7 Mech assault unit and M8 mobile SkyCloud unit)
* **5 Robotic animals** (K-9, M.U.F.F, Phytomining Unit, N.Solution Unit, Chemical Processing Unit)
* **AI SkyCloud supercomputers**: Digitize, store, transfer, and duplicate colonist consciousnesses
* **Remote-controlled surrogate androids**: Safely project minds into expendable mechanical frames
* **New Crafting Materials & Mortar Shells**: Swarm mortar shells that deploy combat nanites
* **Over 70+ parts and implants** for androids and mechanical animals
* **4 Android-specific diseases & maintenance conditions**
* **High-end research tree** and robotic production caskets
* **Custom body layouts & mechanics** (coolant pumps, heat sinks, artificial brains, mechanical organs)
* **AI Persona fabrication & 4 new incidents**

## Special Items & Events

**Mech-Fall Transmitter**: An orbital targeter needed to spawn the M7 Mech. Obtained from item stash quests and orbital traders, similar to other orbital strike weapons.

**The Fractal Enhancement**: A special pill developed by ancient archotech societies. When consumed by an android, it permanently increases nearly every useful stat per use. However, if consumed by a biological human, they will undergo horrific conversion into a fractal mutant.

**Android Assembly Kits**: Compact assembly kits that weigh only a fraction of the fully built android, allowing rapid field deployment and colony founding anywhere.

**Swarm Mortar**: A mortar loaded with specialized shells that deploy a swarm of combat nanobots to aggressively attack hostiles on sight.

**Mechanite Storage Upgrades**: Multi-tiered nanite banks that adjust repair and healing speed, with archotech-grade storage halving the time required to recover from structural damage.

**SkyCloud**: Supercomputing architecture that allows digitizing colonist minds, storing them indefinitely, and running full remote operations across the entire planetary map.

**M8 Mech Unit**: A mobile SkyCloud core equipped with a long-range SkyMind relay, storing up to 10 digital minds and broadcasting 3 active SkyMind links on the move.

## TX Series Infiltration Androids

<p align="center">
    <img src="https://i.imgur.com/fbhDqJg.png" alt="Android Tiers TX Serie" />
</p>

Designed specifically for infiltration, diplomacy, and high-hazard reconnaissance missions. Their modified exoskeletons are coated in synthetic rubber skin or living biological tissues. This protects them from rust, improves social impact, and renders them completely immune to EMP pulses and solar flares (though they are more flammable than standard metal chassis).

### TX Series FAQ

***What are the android models ending with an "I" (TX2I, TX2KI, TX3I, TX4I)?***

These are the same internal mechanical frames as their corresponding infiltration models, but without synthetic skin or living tissues. Refurbished specifically for front-line combat as terror infantry rather than covert operations, their raw, unskinned skeletal appearance inspires dread and strikes fear into enemy forces.

***Is this mod compatible with Questionable Ethics Enhanced (QEE)?***

Yes! When Questionable Ethics Enhanced (QEE) is loaded, TX models with living skin (TX2K, TX3, TX4) can only be created by first manufacturing an inactive mechanical frame at the robotic production casket, then loading that frame into a QEE Cloning/Organ Vat to grow biological tissues over it. This provides a realistic bio-synthetic manufacturing workflow.

***How do I put a TX frame into a QEE clone vat?***

1. Build a TX2K, TX3, or TX4 frame at the robotic production casket.
2. Select the QEE Clone Vat, click **"Start growing"**, and choose the frame from the float menu list.
3. **Appearance Customization**: You can customize your TX android's visual appearance (hair style, hair color, and skin tone) directly using the settings on the Clone Vat **BEFORE** clicking "Start growing".
4. **Developer Mode Note**: If you spawn a TX frame via Dev Mode for testing, spawn an **"Inactivated TX..."** item, rather than directly spawning a "Male/Female TX... frame".

## Mechanical Animals

**K-9 Unit**<br/>
![K-9](https://i.imgur.com/KTQWWFi.png)<br/>
*Summary*: A swift, heavily armored cybernetic canine even without bionic enhancements. Ideal for intercepting attackers and hunting before colonists need to engage.

**M.U.F.F. Unit**<br/>
![M.U.F.F Unit](https://i.imgur.com/MgDITNZ.png)<br/>
*Summary*: A high-capacity robotic pack animal engineered for caravan hauling and transport, though not suited for combat.

**Phytomining Unit**<br/>
![Phytomining Unit](https://i.imgur.com/79YK5Ji.png)<br/>
*Summary*: Extracts trace metals absorbed by local plant life, refining and expelling them as durable metallic fleece strands. This fleece can be woven into protective clothing or melted down into raw steel at an electric smelter.

**N.Solution Unit**<br/>
![N.Solution Unit](https://i.imgur.com/bD4hyrK.png)<br/>
*Summary*: Produces nutrient solution—a rich artificial milk holding twice the nutritional value of standard biological milk.

**Chemical Processing Unit**<br/>
![Chemical Processing Unit](https://i.imgur.com/mugMPOk.png)<br/>
*Summary*: Dispenses up to 5 chemical capsules every 2 days containing semi-refined compounds that can be synthesized into chemfuel or neutroamine at a drug lab or refinery.
